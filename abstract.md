**Title.** Bedside Brief: steering AI pre-encounter recommendations toward the bedside — technical validation on a frozen synthetic benchmark

**Background.** Inpatient work-ups are increasingly planned from the screen rather than the bedside. Generic large language models (LLMs) list history, examination and tests on request but volunteer diagnostic-accuracy figures that cannot be audited. Bedside Brief turns a one-line admission summary into a short card of bedside questions and manoeuvres whose evidence comes only from human-verified records.

**Innovation.** The LLM parses the one-liner and ranks candidates; it never emits sensitivities, likelihood ratios or citations. Retrieval, rendering and a validator that blocks any number absent from a linked verified record are deterministic code. Records hold multiple population-specific estimates, may be "not quantified", and link every claim to its source location. Each record was extracted twice by independent agents, diffed, red-teamed, and promoted only by the author after reading the source.
