## Batch verdict (wave 4 vs wave 1 → wave 3)

The hardened prompt worked. Two failure modes that recurred through waves 1-3 are essentially gone in wave 4: **AUTHOR MISMATCH** (9 wrong lists in wave 1, 3 distinct errors across 6 packets in wave 3, **zero** in 34 spot-checked PMIDs here — including an 8-author Kiriyama citation and a Dubé/Crozier/Middleton/Best/Ohle list neither extractor had reason to have memorized) and **literal `lr = 0` / range-as-point / unlabelled composites** (all present in wave 1 or 3, all absent or down to isolated, well-disclosed instances here). COMPOSITE labelling in particular went from "recurred but less often" (wave 3) to a clean sweep — every composite in this batch, across both extractors, carries the prefix. The RANGES rule is also fully internalized: three different range-only sources (Safdar, Fortes/DRIE, Swap & Nagurney) were each correctly left unconverted with the range quoted in notes.

New watch items introduced for this wave (PROGNOSTIC discipline, in-sample-Youden cut-offs, exposure-window mismatches) show the rules generalizing unevenly: the Katz 1996 and carotid-sinus-massage and Stemmer-sign and ESCAPE-Table-5 traps were all caught by at least one extractor with an explicit, correct disclosure — genuinely sophisticated catches (independent 2x2 recomputation from a mislabelled abstract, reciprocal-LR detection, exposure-window scope caveats) that go beyond what wave 1-3 packets attempted.
